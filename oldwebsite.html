Cuisine

Baguette

Even though this is a famous type of bread in France, it was actually brought to France by an Austrian man named August Zang.

He would bring this long bread from his home city of Vienna, which was the capital of the Austrian Empire at the time. This bread was easy for bakers to flatten and bake.

French Onion Soup

French Onion Soup is a delicious savory dish featuring, of course, onions. It traces back to the 18th century and originated in Paris.

It is made from caramelized onions in a rich beef broth, then topped with bread and melted cheese

Macaron

Macarons are very sweet colorful sandwich cookies that have a crispy shell and a creamy filling.

Although, they are a very popular desert treat in France and generally are known as a French desert

they were actually created somewhere in either Italy or the Middle East, but they were modernized in France.

Cities

Paris

Paris is an iconic city, being the second most visited city in the world and the most visited city in Europe.

Paris is the capital of France and features the iconic structure, the Eiffel Tower.

It is also the biggest city in France by population and is home to the famous Louvre Museum.

Marseille

Marseille is a major port city in france, being the biggest so far.

It was founded around 600 B.C. by Greek settlers

Marseille is the second oldest city in France.

Lyon

Lyon is the 3rd largest city in all of France.

It is considered the gastromonic capital of France as it has amazing cuisine.

It is situated at the confluence Rhone and Saone rivers.
